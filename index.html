About Me

"Hello! I’m Faraz — a dedicated Front-End Developer and tech enthusiast based in Iran. My journey into the world of technology and web development comes with a unique background. As someone with a professional background in healthcare and nursing studies, I’ve always valued precision, empathy, empathy-driven problem-solving, and attention to detail. Over time, my deep curiosity about how digital systems work and how to build things from scratch inspired me to transition into programming. Today, I channel that same discipline and dedication into writing clean, scalable, and efficient code. I specialize in modern front-end technologies, focusing primarily on semantic HTML5, responsive and creative CSS3 (including custom layouts, variables, and modern styling techniques), and dynamic JavaScript. I love bridging the gap between design and functionality—turning raw ideas into polished, responsive, and user-friendly web interfaces that look and work great across all devices. Beyond just writing code, I am a continuous learner. Whether I’m exploring advanced JavaScript concepts, experimenting with UI/UX design patterns, or building practical projects like Faraz-Solutions, I am always striving to push my boundaries and improve my craft. My goal is to build meaningful web experiences, keep growing as a developer, and eventually contribute to impactful tech projects that simplify people’s lives."
